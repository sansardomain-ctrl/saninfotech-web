Our Process

01

Discover

Understand your goals, requirements, and constraints. We explore the problem space before proposing any solution.

02

Plan

Define the approach, scope, technical architecture, and roadmap. Agree on milestones and success criteria.

03

Design

Create intuitive, accessible experiences and information architecture. Validate direction before code is written.

04

Develop

Build, test, and refine the solution with regular reviews, clean engineering practices, and continuous feedback.

05

Deliver

Deploy, document, and support the agreed solution. Stay involved to ensure smooth adoption and ongoing success.

FAQ

Questions, Answered.

Common questions from students, colleges, and businesses considering SAN Info Tech for training, internships, workshops, or custom software development.

What services does SAN Info Tech provide?

SAN Info Tech provides technology training, software development, AI solutions, mobile app development, web design, cloud and DevOps engineering, college internships, technical workshops, and tailored digital solutions for businesses. Our services cover the full journey — from learning the technology to building and shipping real products.

Which technologies can students learn?

Students can explore full-stack web development, Python and Django, Java, Angular and frontend engineering, React, AI and machine learning, generative AI and AI application development, data science and analytics, cloud computing and DevOps, UI/UX design, cybersecurity fundamentals, and database and API development. Each area is presented as a learning path, not a confirmed course with fixed durations or fees.

Do you offer internships for college students?

Yes. SAN Info Tech offers structured technical internship programs with practical project experience, domain-specific training, mentorship, and team-based development exposure. Students can apply through the internship enquiry form on this page. Specific details around duration, format, and availability are confirmed directly with applicants.

Can colleges organize workshops with SAN Info Tech?

Yes. Colleges and universities can collaborate with SAN Info Tech to organize technical workshops, hands-on coding sessions, AI and emerging technology awareness programs, hackathons, coding competitions, technical bootcamps, faculty development sessions, and final-year project mentoring. Use the institutional enquiry form to share your goals and program type.

Can businesses request custom software development?

Absolutely. Businesses can request custom websites, web applications, mobile apps, dashboards, APIs, AI integrations, e-commerce solutions, and tailored business software. Every engagement starts with understanding your actual operational needs before proposing a technical approach — reach out through the contact section to begin the conversation.

Do you provide final-year project guidance?

Yes. SAN Info Tech provides mentored support for final-year academic projects — from concept definition and architecture to implementation, testing, and presentation. Students work with technical mentors and follow real engineering practices rather than just producing a deliverable for submission.

How can I enquire about training programs?

You can use the training enquiry option inside the contact form on this page, call or WhatsApp +91 93635 53032, or reach out through any of the enquiry forms on this site. Share your name, contact details, and preferred technology area, and our team will get back to you with relevant information about the training path you're interested in.

How can a college collaborate with SAN Info Tech?

Colleges can collaborate through workshops, internship programs, faculty development sessions, bootcamps, hackathons, and final-year project mentoring. Department heads, faculty members, or student associations can use the institutional enquiry form on the College Programs section to share college details, contact information, and the type of program they'd like to organize.
