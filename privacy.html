Privacy Policy

How SAN Info Tech collects, uses, and protects information you share with us through this website and our enquiry forms.

Last updated: October 2026

SAN Info Tech ("we", "us", or "our") operates https://www.saninfotechai.in/ (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide through our enquiry, internship application, college collaboration, and contact forms. This may include:

  • Contact details — full name, email address, phone number.
  • Academic or institutional details — college name, contact person, program type.
  • Enquiry details — preferred technology, enquiry category, and your message.
  • Technical metadata — basic browser and device information collected automatically when you visit the Site.
Demo forms notice: The enquiry forms on this Site are currently frontend demonstrations. Submissions are not stored on a server until a backend or third-party form service is configured. Please do not include sensitive personal data (such as government IDs) in any form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries about training, internships, workshops, or software development.
  • Process internship applications and college collaboration requests.
  • Improve our website, training programs, and services.
  • Communicate with you about your enquiry or relevant updates, where you have consented.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies and Tracking

This Site does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking for marketing purposes. If analytics are added in the future, we will update this policy and ensure analytics operate with appropriate privacy controls, in line with applicable law.

4. Data Retention

Once a backend or form service is configured, we will retain enquiry-related data only for as long as necessary to respond to your request and for a reasonable period thereafter, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of any future communications from us.
  • Withdraw consent to processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through the contact form on the homepage.

6. Third-Party Links

This Site may contain links to third-party websites (such as font providers or hosting services). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

7. Children's Privacy

Our training programs and internships are designed primarily for college students and adult learners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information shared through this Site. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
